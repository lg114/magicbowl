Welcome to my Magicbowl. This is not a simple personal website, but a symbolic bowl — a container that holds everything I have: code and projects, reading and thought, sweat on the gym floor, collisions on the snooker table, creations in game worlds, and those fleeting yet enduring insights. Magicbowl is not a static warehouse, but a dynamic magical vessel — the fragments put into it undergo an alchemical transformation through time, reflection, and action.

## 1. Bowl: Containment and Convergence

The bowl has been a symbol of containment and carrying since ancient times in Eastern culture. It can hold rice, water, medicine, and in religion and folk customs, it becomes a vessel for prayers and spells. In my Magicbowl, I gather all the seemingly fragmented contents of life:

- **Technology and Creation:** RagMate, my RAG project, is my current passion for AI/LLMs; the first 2D game I made with the Godot engine is a seed planted in college that finally sprouted.
- **Body and Senses:** Back workouts at the gym let me feel the accumulation of strength; the moment a snooker ball drops into the pocket brings pure satisfaction.
- **Mind and Reading:** Hermann Hesse's *Siddhartha* made me finish it in two days — after closing the book, I felt I had grasped something, and yet nothing at all; Mo Yan's *Life and Death Are Wearing Me Out* made me follow animals through reincarnations to witness human suffering, but I chose to pause at the dog's chapter, because some heaviness needs time to digest.

These contents are not strictly categorized and stored in isolation, but naturally mixed and blended. The philosophy of Bowl is: life is inherently multifaceted, and there is no need to force a single narrative framework. It is like Zhuangzi's "Equality of Things" — all things are equal, and all can enter the bowl; and like the Buddhist concept of "emptiness" — the bowl itself is empty, and it is precisely because of its emptiness that it can contain all things.

## 2. Magic: Flow, Transformation, and Alchemy

A mere "bowl" is just storage; "Magic" gives it the power of wondrous transformation. This is the core of my philosophical thinking: everything put into the bowl is quietly changing.

When reading *Siddhartha*, Siddhartha realized by the river: "The river knows everything." I likewise regard my own experiences as a river, and Magicbowl is the container that observes and converges this river. The discipline from the gym subtly influences the focus I bring to coding; the precision and patience of snooker can be transformed into the calmness needed when debugging; the philosophical musings from reading inject a deeper humanistic dimension into technical projects.

This reminds me of Heraclitus's "everything flows" — nothing is static. The contents of the bowl are not dead matter, but living potential. A reading note may become a blog post months later; a game prototype may evolve into a more complex work; the exhaustion and satisfaction after a workout become a simple confirmation that "a healthy body brings a better life."

Magic also means accepting uncertainty. Just like the feeling after reading *Siddhartha*: you think you've grasped the truth, but it slips through your fingers. This is not failure — it's the bowl reminding you that true wisdom lies in staying open, letting the bowl continue to flow and continue to contain.

## 3. Personal Existence: I Am Magicbowl

Ultimately, Magicbowl is not just a collection of content, but a vessel for my self-narrative. It answers an eternal philosophical question: "Who am I?"

I am not a single label — not just a developer, a fitness enthusiast, or a reader — but a whole that is being constructed. Through this website, I converge my scattered selves and give them unified meaning. Technology is not a cold tool, but my way of "building meaningful products"; reading is not pastime, but a mirror that helps me "think more clearly about life"; physical practice is an anchor grounding me in reality.

This connects to existentialism: Sartre said "existence precedes essence" — we define ourselves through action. Magicbowl is the record and stage of my actions — what I put in, it reflects back, and in the process slowly shapes my essence.

> The river knows everything. I am becoming part of it.

At the same time, it carries the wisdom of Daoist "wu wei": I don't need to deliberately pursue perfection or completeness, I just need to continuously put genuine content into it, and the Magic will naturally happen.

## Conclusion: An Invitation to Enter My Bowl

Magicbowl is not a closed private space, but an open invitation. Welcome to see what I've put in it, and I invite you to think about what's in your own "bowl."

In this rapidly changing era, we all need such a container: one that can both embrace the richness and chaos of life, and let the inner magic slowly ferment. May your bowl always stay open, flowing, and full of wonder.
